---
title: Wednesday Night Ride (WNR)
section: wnr
---

- Weekly ride of 50-60 km
- A/B split depending on turnout
- A group pace is 29-30 km/h pace. B group is 27 km/h.
- "Drop ride" with 2-3 rendezvous points
- Routes rotate weekly between flat and hilly
- Starred Segments will be ID’d on route as target KOMs
- No fuel stops planned (heat dome exceptions)
- Leaves Wednesday evening at 5:30pm from Pandosy Waterfront Park
