Tempo Cycling Club is a Kelowna-based cycling club that promotes high performance while maintaining a safe and respectful group dynamic. This is a place for riders who want to challenge themselves and get better while building connections with like-minded riders. Our members know how to hold a wheel, ride predictably, keep the group together and call out hazards. If this is your vibe then you should [Join Us](#join-us).

The Wednesday Night Ride (WNR) is our backbone, a drop-ride where we push the pace and each other. Once a month, we take it a bit easier with a Social Ride—more conversational, more relaxed, and built around the community. A few times a year, we go big with Marquee Rides—epic distances, memorable destinations, and beautiful scenery. [Learn More](#our-rides)
