---
title: Code of Conduct
section: conduct
---

- Be respectful, we’re all here for a good time
- Use callouts (car back/up, pothole spotting)
- Be predictable. No divebombing corners, no chopping wheels.
- Obey red lights, no crossing over the yellow line
- Groups riding at 2 wide max
- Rear/Front lights required - ride ends when the sun sets
- Shaved legs not required but strongly encouraged
