---

1. Join for up to two trial rides. [Register with CCN](https://ccnbikes.com/#!/events/tempo-cycling-club-2026) and select the “Meet and Greet” option.
2. If we're a good match then [Sign Up](https://ccnbikes.com/#!/events/tempo-cycling-club-2026) and select “Cycling For All Insurance” (or similar) and join us regularly.
3. Membership cost is $50 per year. We're an NPO and membership fees only cover the administration and operating costs of the club.
